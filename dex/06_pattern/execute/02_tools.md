## What tools enable execution patterns?

The tools for enabling Execution Patterns are focused on **"Structural Reference and Reuse."** We use the **DEX Spine itself** as our primary pattern catalog. By organizing the spine into 126 small, highly-cross-referenced files, we create a "Searchable Knowledge Graph" of our proven shapes. This ensures that any human or agent can quickly find and reference the relevant pattern for their current task. The spine is its own "Documentation Tool."

We also rely on **Cross-Reference Links** as a core pattern-enabling tool. By mandating that every file contains links to its siblings, parents, and root documents, we create a "Physical Web" that enforces the system's pattern resonance. These links are the "Enforcers of Fractality," ensuring that every atomic artifact is grounded in the universal patterns of the system. We treat our internal linking as a "Structural Requirement," not just a navigation feature.

Finally, we use **Template Repositories** to manage the "Evolution of Shapes." When a pattern is improved or a new one is identified, we update our core templates and orchestrator rules. These repositories are the "Registry of Excellence," providing the blueprints that agents use to build the system's physical form. The tools are chosen to support the value of "Composability"—they are the mechanisms that ensure every new part of the DEX system is a perfect component of the whole.
