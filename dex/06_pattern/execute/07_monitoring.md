## How do we track?

Monitoring for the Pattern layer is focused on **"Pattern Drift and Resonance."** We track the "Fidelity of Application"—how closely new builds follow established patterns. If we see a pattern being "Modified in the Field" frequently, it flags a "Resonance Gap." We use automated script logs and human review feedback to maintain a real-time view of the "Structural Health" of the entire DEX system.

We also monitor **"Pattern Efficiency."** By analyzing the "Time-to-Approval" for projects using different patterns, we can see which shapes are the most effective at reducing human review overhead. This "Process Intelligence" allows us to identify when a pattern is becoming a "Hollow Ritual" that provides no tangible quality value. Monitoring is our "Sensory Loop" for the system's architectural evolution.

Finally, we track **"Cross-Layer Pattern Consistency."** By running periodic "Geometry Audits" across L0, L1, and L2, we can see if the system's "Self-Similarity" is being maintained. A high consistency score confirms that our patterns are effectively "Syncing the Spine." Monitoring is the "Governance Intelligence" that allows our library of proven wisdom to remain relevant and resilient.
