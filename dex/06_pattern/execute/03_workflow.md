## What is the process?

The workflow for Execution Patterns is a **"Observe-Abstract-Document-Apply-Refine"** cycle. It begins with **"Observation,"** where a human operator identifies a successful structural solution in an application layer (L1/L2). The second stage is **"Abstraction,"** where the solution is stripped of its subject-specific details and reduced to its universal shape. This is the "Pattern Discovery" stage of the workflow.

The third stage is **"Documentation,"** where the abstracted pattern is recorded in the L0 Patterns layer using the standard `dexfilter` methodology. The fourth stage is **"Application,"** where the pattern is "Fitted" into our core templates and orchestrator rules. This ensures that the new wisdom is instantly available to all builders. The final stage is **"Refinement,"** where the pattern is periodically reviewed and updated based on its performance in the field.

This workflow is designed to be **"Non-Destructive and Additive."** We prioritize "Capturing what works" and use the 8:3:8 law to resolve any pattern conflicts. By following this cycle, we ensure that the system's "Evolutionary Engine" produces a library of patterns that are both practical and high-fidelity. The workflow is the "Pulse" of the DEX system's architectural evolution.
