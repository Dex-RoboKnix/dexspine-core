## How do we verify?

Validation of the Pattern layer is a **"Structural Resonance Check."** The primary verification step is to ensure that a pattern, when applied to a new domain, produces high-quality results. We use **"Cross-Domain Testing"**—applying a pattern (like the 19-point filter) to three diverse subjects (e.g., software architecture, business policy, and personal knowledge) and auditing the outcomes. If the pattern produces consistent, irreducible results in all three, it is validated.

We also perform **"Pattern-Grounding Audits."** This involves checking that every pattern in this layer is consistent with the underlying layers (01-05). We look for "Pattern Contradictions"—solutions that seem effective but violate a core principle or policy. If a pattern cannot be "Grounding Checked" against the rest of the spine, it is flagged for refinement. This ensures the "Fractal Integrity" of the system.

Finally, we validate through **"Agent Intuition Surveys."** We ask agents to identify the "Most Helpful Patterns" during a complex build. If multiple agents independently cite the same pattern as a key factor in their success, the pattern is validated. This "Model Feedback" is essential for identifying patterns that are naturally aligned with the way LLMs process information. Validation is the "Quality Gate" that ensures our patterns are truly "Reusable Wisdom."
