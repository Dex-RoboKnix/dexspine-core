## What does execution patterns need?

The Pattern layer is dependent on **All Previous Layers (01-05)**. Patterns do not exist in a vacuum; they are the "Emergent Properties" of a well-grounded system. A pattern like "Filter Sliding" depends on the procedures for file creation (Layer 05), the staged hierarchy of the build process (Layer 04), the governance of the policy layer (Layer 03), and the behavioral constraints of the principles layer (Layer 02). Patterns need the "Underlying Strata" to be stable for them to emerge.

Another critical dependency is **Cross-Project Observation**. Patterns are identified by comparing successful builds across different subjects and domains. This requires a library of completed "Case Studies" and project logs. If the system only ever builds one thing, it can never identify a pattern. Patterns depend on the "Structural Variety" of the applications they govern.

Finally, patterns depend on **Human Abstraction (Layer 8)**. While an LLM can match patterns, the "Identification and Naming" of a new DEX pattern requires human judgment and intuitive leap. We must see that a specific solution is not just "locally effective" but is "universally applicable." Patterns need the "High-Level Synthesis" capability of the human operator to be codified into the L0 foundation.
