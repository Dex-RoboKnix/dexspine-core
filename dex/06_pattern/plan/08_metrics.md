## How do we measure execution patterns?

We measure the effectiveness of the Pattern layer using three primary metrics. First is the **"Pattern Adoption Rate."** We track how often documented patterns are correctly identified and applied in new `spec.md` files and project tracks. A high adoption rate confirms that our patterns are "Accessible and Resonant" for builders. If agents and humans are frequently ignoring a pattern in favor of custom solutions, it indicates that the pattern is either poorly documented or ineffective.

The second metric is **"Success Correlation."** We compare the "First-Time Approval Rate" of projects that explicitly follow established patterns versus those that don't. A strong positive correlation confirms the "Quality Value" of our patterns. We aim for a state where "Following the Pattern" is the most reliable predictor of project success. Pattern metrics are our "Proof of Wisdom."

Finally, we track **"Pattern Evolution Latency."** This measures the time it takes for a successful new solution identified in an application layer (L1/L2) to be abstracted and codified in the L0 Pattern layer. We aim for a "Responsive Evolution"—where the system is quickly learning from its successes and updating its foundational library. Monitoring these metrics ensures that our Patterns layer remains a "Living Archive" of excellence.
