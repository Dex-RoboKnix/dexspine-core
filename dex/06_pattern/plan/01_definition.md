## What IS execution patterns of the DEX system?

Execution Patterns in the DEX system are the reusable solutions and structural templates that emerge from repeated success. They represent the "Wisdom of the System," capturing the best ways to solve recurring architectural and operational problems. While Procedures (Layer 05) provide atomic steps, Patterns provide the "Higher-Order Logic" that allows those steps to be combined into complex, coherent outcomes.

A core DEX pattern is the **"Triple Lens: Default + DEX + Null."** This pattern defines how any subject should be analyzed through three distinct perspectives to ensure 360-degree coverage. Another fundamental pattern is **"Filter Sliding,"** which involves applying the same 19-point analytical filter to different subjects across the system. We also utilize various **Track Types** (linear, parallel, diamond, iterative) to manage different kinds of development workflows.

Patterns are the "Evolutionary Memory" of the spine. They are not just rules, but "Proven Shapes" that have been battle-tested in real-world human-LLM collaborations. By documenting these patterns at the L0 layer, we ensure that as the system grows, it continues to evolve in a way that is consistent with its most successful historical states. Patterns are the "Fractal Blueprints" of the DEX infrastructure.
