## How do we know it works?

Success for the Pattern layer is defined by **"Patterns Applied Without Conscious Effort."** If a structural solution (like the 19-point filter) is used consistently across multiple project tracks because it has become the "Natural Way" of building, the pattern has succeeded. Success is the "Invisibility of Excellence"—where the best way to do something is so obvious and well-supported that no one even considers an alternative.

Another success criterion is **"Pattern Reuse Frequency."** We track how often our documented patterns are referenced and applied in new domains (L1/L2). A high reuse frequency confirms that our patterns are truly "Universal" and provide real value to builders. If a pattern is documented but never used, it has failed its success criteria and should be audited for simplification or removal.

Finally, success is measured by **"New Pattern Proposal Rate."** A healthy system is one where new patterns are constantly being identified from successful executions. If the community is actively "Mining the application layers" for new foundational patterns, it demonstrates that the system's "Evolutionary Engine" is working. Success is a system that is not just "using patterns," but is "generating patterns" as a byproduct of its own growth.
