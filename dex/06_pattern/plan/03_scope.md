# Pattern — Scope

**Spine:** DEX  
**Layer:** 06_pattern  
**Phase:** Plan

---

## What is included and excluded?

The scope of the Patterns layer includes all repeatable, high-level structures and templates used across the DEX system. This covers the "Analytical Patterns" (Triple Lens, Filter Sliding), the "Orchestration Patterns" (Track Types, Diamond Flow), and the "Fractal Patterns" (the filter defining itself). It also includes the high-level "Documentation Patterns" that govern how we organize our L0, L1, and L2 layers.

Included in the scope are the "Meta-Patterns"—the patterns that define how other patterns are identified, documented, and applied. This ensures that the system's "Pattern Recognition" capability is itself a structured part of the spine. Any structural solution that has been proven effective in more than one project track is a candidate for inclusion in the scope of Layer 06.

Excluded from the scope are one-off solutions, specific technical implementations, or atomic procedures (Layer 05). For example, the *pattern* of using a 19-point filter is in Layer 06, but the specific *procedure* for creating one of those 19 files is in Layer 05. Similarly, the *pattern* of sequential execution is in Layer 06, but the *policy* of when to stop that sequence for approval is in Layer 03. Pattern is the "Structural Archetype" layer.
