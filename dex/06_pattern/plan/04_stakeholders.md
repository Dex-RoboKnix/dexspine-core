## Who cares about execution patterns?

The primary stakeholders for the Pattern layer are the **System Designers and Architects**. They care about patterns because they are the "Building Blocks" of the system's architecture. By identifying and documenting successful patterns, architects can ensure that the system's growth is managed and that new components are built on a solid, proven foundation. Patterns are the primary tools for "Managing Complexity" at the system level.

The second primary stakeholders are the **Future Builders and Community Contributors**. For them, patterns provide the "Learning Path" for the system. Instead of having to learn every atomic procedure from scratch, they can learn the high-level patterns (like "Filter Sliding") and use them to understand the system's overall logic. Patterns provide the "Cognitive Context" that allows new contributors to be effective quickly.

Finally, **LLM Agents (Layer 7)** are key stakeholders. While agents do not "care" in a human sense, they are highly sensitive to pattern consistency. By providing agents with explicit structural patterns, we allow them to use their "Inherent Pattern Matching" capability to produce more aligned and reliable results. A system with clear, consistent patterns is much easier for an AI to parse and execute than one with arbitrary or inconsistent structures.
