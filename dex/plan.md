# DEXSPINE — Plan Phase

**Spine:** DEX  
**Phase:** Plan  
**Status:** Operational  

---

## Overview

The Plan phase is the first stage of the `dexfilter` application. It focuses on defining the "what," "why," and "who" of each layer. By answering eight fundamental questions, we establish a grounded understanding of the subject before proceeding to extraction or execution.

### The 8 Plan Questions:
1. **Definition:** What IS the subject?
2. **Purpose:** WHY does the subject exist?
3. **Scope:** What is included and excluded?
4. **Stakeholders:** Who cares about the subject?
5. **Success Criteria:** How do we know it works?
6. **Dependencies:** What does the subject need?
7. **Constraints:** What limits the subject?
8. **Metrics:** How do we measure the subject?

## Connections

The Plan phase provides the conceptual foundation for the entire DEX system. It feeds directly into the **MVE Extraction** (to find the essence) and the **Execute Phase** (to define implementation). A complete Plan is required before execution can be considered validated.
