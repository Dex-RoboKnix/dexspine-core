## How do we improve?

Improvement of the Process layer is a **"Workflow Optimization"** process. We use the data from our "Post-Project Retrospectives" to identify stages of the hierarchy that are causing unnecessary friction or rework. We acknowledge that our initial processes are the 99.99% state, and we are committed to the 0.01% refinements that make the "DEX Build" more efficient. This is achieved through **Process Retrospectives**, where we analyze our build velocity and error rates.

We improve through **"Model-Process Tuning."** As new LLM models are released, we update our "Economic Model Cascade" to incorporate them. We test their performance on specific stages of the DEX build and adjust the process rules to ensure we are always using the "Best Tool for the Step." This "Compute Adaptation" ensures that our "Operating Engine" remains at the cutting edge of what's possible.

Finally, we improve by **"Fractal Process Extraction."** As we apply the `dexfilter` to new domains (L1/L2), we discover new, more efficient ways of organizing work. We abstract these "Local Improvements" back into the L0 Process layer, ensuring that the entire ecosystem benefits from the "Collective Intelligence" of our builders. Improvement is the mechanism that ensures our "Workflow Orchestration" remains the "Universal Map of Progress" for the entire system.
