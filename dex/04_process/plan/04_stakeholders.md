## Who cares about system processes?

The primary stakeholders for the Process layer are the **Human Operators (Layer 8)** and **Project Managers**. They rely on processes to track progress, manage expectations, and ensure that the build is moving according to the plan. For the human operator, processes provide the "Dashboard" of the project—the high-level view of what has been completed and what is coming next. A clear process reduces the cognitive burden of project management.

The second primary stakeholders are the **LLM Agents (Layer 7)**. Agents use processes as their "Lifecycle Guide." By following the established workflows (e.g., "Always write the plan before the execute files"), an agent can maintain a consistent approach even across multiple sessions. Processes provide the "Structure of Work" that prevents agents from becoming overwhelmed by large task lists. Agents are the primary *executors* of the process layer.

Finally, **Future Contributors and System Architects** are key stakeholders. They care about processes because they define how the system evolves and how new components are integrated. Standardized processes ensure that the "DEX Quality" is maintained as the system scales and that new contributors can quickly "get into the flow" of the project. Processes are the "SOPs" of the DEX infrastructure.
