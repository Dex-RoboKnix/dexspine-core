## WHY does system processes of the DEX system exist?

The primary purpose of System Processes is to prevent waste and ensure architectural consistency. In a rapid development environment involving AI agents, there is a constant temptation to skip stages or jump straight to execution. Processes provide the "Forced Discipline" that ensures the foundation is solid before the walls are built. By mandating a staged approach (like the 5 phases of this build), we prevent the "Rework Spiral" that occurs when early conceptual errors go unnoticed.

Processes also exist to optimize resource allocation. The "Economic Model Cascade" policy is implemented through a process that ensures we use the most cost-effective and efficient model for each task. This ensures that the system remains sustainable and that our compute budget is focused on where it provides the most value. Processes are the "Resource Management" layer of the system.

Finally, processes enable "Seamless Handoffs." By standardizing the stages of work and the artifacts produced at each stage (e.g., `spec.md`, `CASESTUDY.md`), we ensure that any agent or human can pick up a project at any point and understand exactly where it stands. Processes create the "Universal Language of Progress" that allows for long-duration, multi-session collaboration without context loss.
