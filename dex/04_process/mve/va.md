## Minimum Viable Essence

The core value driving the Process layer is **"Process Prevents Waste."** We believe that a disciplined, staged approach to building is the only way to ensure quality and prevent costly rework. We value "Patience Over Haste," believing that the time spent on a solid spec and plan is returned many times over during execution. This value is the foundation of our "Concept Before Execution" mandate.

We also value **"Invisible Efficiency."** We believe that a good process should be like a well-oiled engine—powerful but quiet. The best process is one that guides the system naturally, without creating unnecessary friction or "busy work" for humans or agents. We strive for workflows that are "Just Right"—providing enough structure to ensure safety, but enough flexibility to allow for high-velocity innovation.

Finally, we hold the value that **"Bad Process is Friction."** We are committed to a continuous improvement loop that identifies and eliminates process overhead. We reject "Process for Process's Sake," instead focusing on workflows that provide a tangible "Return on Grounding." If a process isn't making the system more stable, consistent, or efficient, it has no place in the DEX L0 spine.
