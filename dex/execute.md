# DEXSPINE — Execute Phase

**Spine:** DEX  
**Phase:** Execute  
**Status:** Operational  

---

## Overview

The Execute phase is the final stage of the `dexfilter` application. It focuses on the practical "how" of each layer—defining the tools, workflows, and validation steps required to bring the conceptual plan into reality. 

### The 8 Execute Questions:
1. **Approach:** How do we implement the subject?
2. **Tools:** What tools enable the subject?
3. **Workflow:** What is the process or workflow?
4. **Artifacts:** What gets produced?
5. **Validation:** How do we verify the output?
6. **Rollback:** How do we undo or revert changes?
7. **Monitoring:** How do we track the subject in operation?
8. **Improvement:** How do we improve the subject over time?

## Connections

The Execute phase is the practical implementation of the **Plan** and **MVE** phases. It should not be started until the Plan phase for a given layer is complete. Execution without a Plan is considered a violation of DEX Principles.

For the universal rules governing these questions, see [DEXFILTER.md](../DEXFILTER.md).
