## How do we improve?

Improvement of the Policy layer is driven by **"Exception-Led Evolution."** We acknowledge that our initial policies are the 99.99% state, and we are committed to the 0.01% additions that handle the edge cases we missed. This is achieved through **Post-Project Retrospectives**, where we analyze the `CASESTUDY.md` logs for every escalation, veto, and policy friction. We use these "Data Points of Pain" to refine our existing policies or create new ones.

We also improve by **Optimizing the "Decision Geometry."** We analyze our "Checkpoint Latency" to see if we can move certain approval steps earlier in the process or automate routine verifications that don't require high-level human judgment. The goal is to make our governance "Thinner but Stronger"—reducing the friction for the human operator while increasing the system's overall alignment and safety.

Finally, we improve through **"Fractal Policy Refinement."** As we apply the `dexfilter` to more subjects (L1/L2), we discover new patterns of decision-making that can be abstracted back into the L0 Policy layer. By constantly "Mining the applications for foundation," we ensure that our L0 spine remains the "Sharpest Policy Tool" for the entire ecosystem. Improvement is the mechanism that ensures our "Human Authority" becomes more effective as the system scales.
