## How do we track?

Monitoring for the Policy layer is focused on **"Compliance and Drift Observation."** We don't monitor for technical performance, but for "Governance Health." This is done through **Checkpoint Auditing**, where we track the frequency and success of mandatory stop points. If we see that checkpoints are being "Rubber Stamped" without real review, it flags a "Policy Decay" that must be addressed through human re-engagement.

We also monitor **"Escalation Patterns."** By analyzing the `CASESTUDY.md` for 8:3:8 law invocations, we can see if the system is encountering "Pockets of Ambiguity" that our existing policies aren't handling. This "Drift Monitoring" allows us to identify when the system's "operating reality" is outgrowing its "pre-defined rules." Monitoring is our "Sensory Feedback" for the system's alignment.

Finally, we track **"Veto Frequency and Impact."** We observe how often the Layer 8 Veto is used and what the "cost of correction" is each time. If the veto is being used frequently for the same type of agent error, it indicates that our policies are not providing the agent with enough guidance. Monitoring is the "Governance Intelligence" that allows us to evolve our policies from a grounded, evidence-based perspective.
