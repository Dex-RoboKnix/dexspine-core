## What gets produced?

The primary artifacts produced by the Policy layer are the **19 Filter Files** and the **DEXFILTER_APPLICATION.md** for this layer. These documents are the "Rulebook" for the system, defining how decisions are made and how authority is managed. Each file is a tangible record of a specific policy point (e.g., `01_definition.md` defines what policies are, `mve/hu.md` provides the human justification for them). These files are the source of truth for all "Governance Audits."

Another critical artifact is the **"Escalation Matrix"**—a structured document (or table within a policy file) that defines the specific triggers and paths for ambiguity resolution. This matrix is used by both humans and agents to determine "What do we do now?" when the standard path is blocked. The matrix is a "Navigation Asset" that ensures the system always moves toward a structured outcome.

Finally, this layer produces the **Checkpoint Reports** (recorded in `CASESTUDY.md`). These artifacts are the evidence of the policy layer in action. They capture the specific work submitted for review, the human's feedback, and the final decision. The artifacts of the Policy layer are the "Governance DNA" that ensures the system's L0 foundation remains accountable and human-aligned.
