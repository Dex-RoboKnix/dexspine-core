## Who cares about decision policies?

The primary stakeholder for the Policy layer is the **Human Operator (Layer 8)**. This individual is the ultimate authority in the system and relies on policies to manage their time and ensure the safety of their autonomous agents. For the human, policies are "delegation contracts"—they define what the agent can do on its own and when it must stop to ask for permission. A well-defined policy layer gives the human confidence to let the system run.

The second primary stakeholders are the **LLM Agents (Layer 7)**. Agents use policies as their "Operating System Rules." By reading the policies, an agent understands its boundaries and the triggers for escalation. This reduces "Agent Anxiety" (hallucinated uncertainty) and ensures that the agent's actions are always compliant with the established governance. Agents are the primary *executors* of the policy layer.

Finally, **System Administrators and Security Auditors** are key stakeholders. They care about policies related to data handling, model usage, and resource allocation. Policies provide the "Audit Trail" that these stakeholders use to verify that the system is operating within legal and ethical boundaries. In an enterprise or community setting, policies are the "SLA" between the system and its users.
