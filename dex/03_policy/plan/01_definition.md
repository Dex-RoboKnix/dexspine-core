## What IS decision policies of the DEX system?

Decision Policies in the DEX system are the formal frameworks and decision rules that govern how the system operates, resolves conflicts, and manages authority. While Principles (Layer 02) provide behavioral constraints, Policies provide the specific "logic gates" for decision-making. They define the "if-then" scenarios that ensure the system remains aligned with its core values even in complex, multi-variable environments.

A central pillar of DEX Policy is the **Layer 8 Override Capability**. This policy mandates that a human operator always has the final say and can veto any automated action or agent decision. Policy also defines the escalation paths for ambiguity—specifically, when a binary choice fails, the policy dictates the immediate invocation of the `dexfilter` via the 8:3:8 law.

Policies act as the governance layer that sits between the "spirit" of the principles and the "mechanics" of the processes. They are the rules of engagement for AI agents, ensuring that automation serves human ends and that all automated actions are taken with informed consent (either implicit through pre-approved policy or explicit through checkpoints).
