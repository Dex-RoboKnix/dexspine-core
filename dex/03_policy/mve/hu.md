## Minimum Viable Essence

The Policy layer is rooted in the human experience of **"Being Overridden by Automation."** We have all felt the frustration of a system making a choice for us that was wrong, or a tool that changed its behavior without our consent. These experiences taught us the importance of the "Veto" and the "Undo." We recognize that human intuition often captures subtle nuances that automated logic gates miss, and our policies are designed to respect this qualitative "Layer 8" edge.

We also understand the **"Need for Informed Consent."** In our interactions with early AI agents, we found that "Blind Autonomy" led to a breakdown in trust. We felt the need to know *what* the agent was about to do before it did it. This human need for situational awareness is what birthed our checkpoint policies. We value the "Calm" that comes from knowing the system is operating within pre-approved boundaries.

Finally, we value **"Responsibility."** We acknowledge that as the builders of these systems, we are ultimately responsible for their outputs. We cannot blame "the algorithm" for a failure. Our policies are the expression of this responsibility—they are the rules we set for ourselves and our agents to ensure we are always acting with integrity. Policy is the "Human Face" of the system's governance.
