## Minimum Viable Essence

The core value driving the Policy layer is **"Human Authority is Non-Negotiable."** We believe that no matter how advanced an AI agent becomes, it must remain a tool that serves human ends. We value the "Veto" as the ultimate proof of system alignment. This belief mandates that our policies are designed to preserve human control, even at the cost of operational speed.

We also value **"Informed Consent."** We believe that automated systems should never "sneak" changes past a human operator. Every significant action—especially those that modify the file system or consume resources—should be taken with the human's full awareness of the *why* and the *how*. Our policies are the mechanism that ensures this transparency is maintained across all levels of the system.

Finally, we hold the value of **"Deterministic Governance."** We believe that rules should be clear, predictable, and consistently applied. We reject "Ad-Hoc" decision-making in favor of structured policy. This value ensures that the system's behavior is auditable and that agents have a stable framework for their autonomous actions. Governance is not a burden; it is the "Safety Architecture" that makes autonomy possible.
