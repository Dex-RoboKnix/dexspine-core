## What IS core values of the DEX system?

The Core Values of the DEX system are the foundational, non-negotiable beliefs that ground the entire cognitive architecture. They represent the "irreducible essence" of the system's identity and intent. These values are not merely aspirational statements; they are operational constraints that influence every decision, from high-level policy to atomic procedures.

At the heart of DEX is the principle of "Infrastructure, Not Product." This means the system is designed as a stable, vendor-agnostic spine rather than a transient consumer tool. We pursue perfection through the "Asymptotic 100%" rule—acknowledging that while 100% perfection is never reached, the constant addition of structural integrity (99.99% + 0.01%) brings us ever closer to an ideal state of reliability and alignment.

These values also mandate the preservation of human authority (Layer 8 Veto) and the commitment to open, shared knowledge. By encoding these beliefs into the L0 foundation, we ensure that as the system grows and more complex agents (L1, L2) are added, the core remains stable and true to its original human-centered purpose.
