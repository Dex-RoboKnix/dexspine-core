## Who cares about core values?

The primary stakeholders for the Core Values are the human operators (Layer 8) who design and interact with the system. These individuals rely on the values to ensure that the AI agents they employ are aligned with their intent and that the system's evolution remains predictable and safe. Human stakeholders include individual researchers, system architects, and community contributors who build upon the DEX foundation.

Secondary stakeholders are the AI agents themselves (Layer 7). While agents do not "care" in a human sense, they are "stakeholders" because their operational success depends on having a clear, consistent context to parse. By encoding values in the L0 spine, we provide agents with a "moral compass" that allows them to make autonomous decisions that are likely to be approved by the human operator. A well-defined value layer reduces the cognitive load on agents by providing clear boundaries and grounding.

Finally, the broader community and future adopters of the DEX infrastructure are stakeholders. They benefit from a standardized, transparent foundation that allows for knowledge sharing and interoperability. By adhering to a common set of values, different organizations can collaborate on cognitive assets without fearing "vendor lock-in" or "context divergence." The values protect the interests of everyone who uses or contributes to the spine.
