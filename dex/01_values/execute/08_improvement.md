## How do we improve?

Improvement of the Values layer is driven by the "Asymptotic Feedback Loop." We acknowledge that our initial expression of core beliefs is the 99.99% state, and we are committed to the 0.01% additions that bring us closer to the 100%. This is achieved through **Quarterly Value Reviews**, where we analyze the data gathered from monitoring and audits to identify gaps, contradictions, or opportunities for clarification.

We improve the values by **Synthesizing MVE Perspectives**. The human insights (hu.md) provide the intuitive direction, while the LLM analysis (llm.md) provides the structural patterns. By periodically merging these perspectives into the core Plan (01-08) and Execute (01-08) files, we ensure the values remain "vibrant" and grounded in both human meaning and machine logic. This synthesis is the primary mechanism for value evolution.

Finally, we improve through **Community Contribution**. As more people use the DEX spine in diverse projects, they will encounter unique challenges and edge cases that our original values didn't anticipate. By incorporating this external feedback into the L0 foundation, we strengthen the "Universal" nature of the infrastructure. Improvement is not about making the values "bigger," but about making them "more irreducible"—simpler, clearer, and more effective at grounding the system.
