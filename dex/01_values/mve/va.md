## Minimum Viable Essence

The irreducible essence of the DEX system is the belief that "Infrastructure Precedes Tooling." We believe that for AI to be truly beneficial and safe, it must be grounded in a stable, transparent, and human-controllable foundation. We value the "Spine" over the "Application," prioritizing the structural integrity that allows many applications to flourish. This is the core belief that drives the creation of the L0 layer.

We also believe in the "Asymptotic Pursuit of Perfection." We accept that our current systems are imperfect (99.99%), but we are committed to the disciplined, incremental addition of structure (0.01%) to move closer to the 100% state. This value ensures that DEX is never static; it is always evolving through a rigorous, structured process of self-improvement and refinement.

Finally, we hold the value that "Knowledge Must Be Shared." DEX is a vendor-agnostic infrastructure by constitution. We believe that the patterns and principles of cognitive architecture should be open and accessible to everyone, ensuring that human authority (Layer 8) is never compromised by proprietary black boxes or walled gardens.
