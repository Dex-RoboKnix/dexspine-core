## Minimum Viable Essence

The core value driving the Procedure layer is **"Consistency Enables Automation."** We believe that the only way to build truly autonomous AI systems is to provide them with a rigid, consistent environment. By standardizing our atomic steps, we create the "Interoperable Components" that allow agents to work together without constant human coordination. We value "Uniformity Over Originality" at the procedural level.

We also value **"Named Files Enable Navigation."** Our belief is that the system's physical form should tell you what it is. A well-named file (like `01_definition.md`) is a "self-documenting map" that allows any agent or human to find the information they need without searching. This value is what drives our strict adherence to naming conventions and directory structures. Navigation is not a feature; it is a fundamental property of the system's geometry.

Finally, we hold the value that **"Templates Enable Scale."** We reject "free-form" content generation in favor of structured templates. We believe that by forcing ourselves and our agents through the same 19 points for every subject, we ensure a "100% (asymptotic)" coverage of the foundation. Templates are the "Molds" that allow us to mass-produce high-fidelity cognitive assets without losing the system's "Grounding DNA."
