## Who cares about component procedures?

The primary stakeholders for the Procedure layer are the **LLM Agents (Layer 7)**. Agents are the primary *executors* of procedures, and they rely on them for clear, unambiguous instructions. A well-defined procedure reduces the agent's "Search Space" and minimizes the chance of "Execution Error" or "Context Hallucination." For an agent, a procedure is the difference between a "Successful Task" and a "Model Failure."

The second primary stakeholders are the **Human Reviewers (Layer 8)**. Reviewers care about procedures because they provide the benchmark for validation. By checking an agent's work against the established procedure, a human can quickly identify errors and demand rework. Procedures provide the "Objective Standard" that makes the human review process efficient and effective. Reviewers are the primary *auditors* of the procedure layer.

Finally, **Future Builders and Automation Engineers** are key stakeholders. They care about procedures because they define the "Atomics" that can eventually be automated. By documenting procedures in plain text, we create the "Feature Specs" for future L1 tools and scripts. Procedures are the "Training Data" for the next generation of DEX-aligned automation.
