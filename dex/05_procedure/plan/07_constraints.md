## What limits component procedures?

The primary constraint on the Procedure layer is **"Machine-Parseability."** Because procedures are primarily executed by AI agents, they must be written in a way that is unambiguous and easy for a model to interpret. This limits the use of poetic or abstract language. Procedures must be literal, sequential, and follow a strict "input-steps-output" logic. If a procedure requires "human intuition" to be understood, it is a violation of this constraint.

Another constraint is **"Unambiguous Singularity."** A procedure should only ever have one correct output for a given input. We avoid "conditional branching" within procedures wherever possible, preferring instead to break complex tasks into multiple, simpler procedures. This constraint ensures that the system's "Atomic Level" remains irreducible and easy to validate.

Finally, procedures are constrained by **"Template Rigidity."** To maintain the "DEX Style," all procedures must use the established templates provided in the specification. This limits the creative freedom of the agent or human, but it ensures that the resulting artifacts are perfectly consistent. We prioritize "Structural Uniformity" over "Expressionistic Variety," ensuring that the spine remains a professional, high-fidelity infrastructure.
