## How do we measure component procedures?

We measure the health of the Procedure layer using three primary metrics. First is the **"Agent Compliance Rate."** We track how often an AI agent correctly follows the steps of a procedure on its first attempt. A low compliance rate suggests that the procedure is either too vague, too complex, or misaligned with the model's capabilities. We use this data to identify procedures that need to be simplified or better "embedded" in the templates.

The second metric is **"Template Adherence Consistency."** We perform automated checks on the resulting markdown files to ensure they meet 100% of the structural requirements (frontmatter, cross-references, line counts). This is a quantitative measure of "Physical Quality." High consistency scores prove that our procedures are effectively enforcing the "DEX Style" across all project components.

Finally, we track **"Naming and Link Accuracy."** We use scripts to identify broken links or files that do not follow the established naming conventions. A high accuracy score confirms that the "Searchable Geometry" of the spine is being maintained. By quantifying these "Atomic Level Errors," we can continuously refine our procedures to make the system more irreducible and stable.
