## What IS component procedures of the DEX system?

Component Procedures in the DEX system are the specific, step-by-step instructions for discrete tasks. They represent the "atomic" level of work within the infrastructure, providing the detailed "how-to" for repeatable actions. While Processes (Layer 04) provide the high-level workflow, Procedures provide the granular detail required for consistent execution by both humans and LLM agents.

A fundamental principle of DEX procedures is "1 Task = 1 File = 1 Output." This ensures that work is modular, easily auditable, and fits within the context windows of modern AI models. Procedures cover everything from file naming conventions and template usage to specific validation steps and reporting requirements. They are the "checklists" that ensure the system's L0 foundation is built correctly every time.

Procedures are the most numerous and varied parts of the spine. They are the practical components that humans and agents interact with most frequently. By defining these procedures at the L0 layer, we ensure that even the smallest task is grounded in the system's core values and principles, preventing the "procedural drift" that can occur in less structured environments.
