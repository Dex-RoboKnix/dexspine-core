## What gets produced?

The primary artifacts produced by the Procedure layer are the **19 Filter Files** and the **DEXFILTER_APPLICATION.md** for this layer. These documents are the "Instructional Manual" for the system, providing the atomic details for all work. Each file is a tangible record of a specific procedural point (e.g., `01_definition.md` defines what procedures are, `mve/va.md` provides the values justifying them). These files are the "Step-by-Step DNA" of the system.

Beyond the layer-specific files, this layer produces the **Procedure Library**—the collection of all documented SOPs for the DEX system. This library is the primary reference for AI agents, ensuring they have a stable, irreducible set of instructions for every routine task. The library is a "Cognitive Asset" that enables the system's high-velocity building.

Finally, this layer produces the **Validation and Audit Logs** (via `CASESTUDY.md` and script outputs). These artifacts are the evidence that the procedures are being followed. They capture the results of naming checks, link audits, and structural verifications. The artifacts of the Procedure layer are the "Physical Evidence of Quality," ensuring that every component of the spine is built to the highest possible standard.
