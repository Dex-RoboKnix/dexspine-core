## How do we improve?

Improvement of the Procedure layer is a **"Simplify and Automate"** process. We use the data from our execution monitoring to identify procedures that are frequently failing or causing excessive friction. We acknowledge that our initial procedures are the 99.99% state, and we are committed to the 0.01% refinements that make the "Atomic Build" more natural. This is achieved through **Procedural Retrospectives**, where we analyze our error rates and simplify instruction steps.

We also improve through **"Template Optimization."** As we discover better ways to structure information for LLM parsing, we update our core templates. By making the "Physical Geometry" more efficient, we automatically improve the performance of all procedures that use those templates. This "Structural Improvement" ensures that the spine remains at the cutting edge of human-AI collaboration standards.

Finally, we improve by **"Automating the Boring Stuff."** Any procedure that is 100% deterministic and low-risk is a candidate for full automation through L1 tools and scripts. By moving these tasks from "Agent Execution" to "Script Execution," we free up the system's cognitive resources for higher-level work. Improvement is the mechanism that ensures our Procedure layer remains the "Most Efficient Atomic Engine" for building the DEX infrastructure.
