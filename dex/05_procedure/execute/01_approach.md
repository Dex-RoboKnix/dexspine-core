## How do we implement component procedures?

The implementation of Component Procedures follows a **"Define, Embed, and Repeat"** approach. We implement each procedure by defining it as a strict "Input -> Steps -> Output" function. This ensures that the instructions are actionable and testable. We document these procedures directly in the L0 spine (this layer), making them the "Standard Reference" for all system operations.

Second, we **Embed** the procedures into our templates and orchestration tools. When a new project track is created, the relevant procedures are automatically included in the `spec.md` or the /conductor's task list. This ensures that the "Atomic Instructions" are always present at the point of execution, reducing the cognitive load on the agent or human. We don't just "document" procedures; we "deploy" them as part of the system's active context.

Finally, we implement procedures through **"Rigorous Repetition."** We mandate that every task, no matter how simple, must follow the established procedure. This "Procedural Discipline" is what builds the system's "Operational Habit." By repeating the same high-quality steps across all 126 files, we ensure that the entire foundation is built to the same irreducible standard. The approach is focused on "Consistency over Speed," ensuring that every part of the spine is a perfect component of the whole.
