## Minimum Viable Essence

The core value driving the Principles layer is **"Simplicity Through Constraint."** We believe that the best way to manage complexity is not by adding more rules, but by adding the *right* rules—constraints that naturally guide the system toward high-quality, irreducible outcomes. We value the "Elegance of Plain Text" (Markdown) and the "Power of Sequential Progress," believing that doing one thing well at a time is the only way to build a complex, stable foundation.

We also value **"Absolute Transparency."** Our principles demand that nothing be hidden in proprietary code or complex abstractions. Everything—from how we name our files to how we resolve ambiguity—must be visible and auditable by a human. This transparency is the source of our system's integrity and the primary means of ensuring human authority is never bypassed.

Finally, we hold the principle of **"Composability"** as a high value. We believe that a universal spine must be built from modular, interchangeable parts. By adhering to the "1 Task = 1 File" principle, we create a system that is easy to navigate, easy to test, and easy for any agent to join. Composability is what allows the DEX infrastructure to scale without becoming brittle or unmanageable.
