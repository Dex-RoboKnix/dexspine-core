## What does fundamental principles need?

The Principles layer is heavily dependent on the **Values Layer (Layer 01)**. Principles have no authority of their own; their legitimacy is entirely derived from the core beliefs they represent. If the values are unclear or contradictory, the principles will be equally flawed. The "Grounding Chain" requires that every principle can be traced back to at least one foundational value.

Another critical dependency is the **8:3:8 Law of Reduction by Addition**. Principles are the primary subjects of the `dexfilter` applications in the L0 spine. The filter is the mechanism that ensures the principles themselves are irreducible, consistent, and well-defined. Without the law, our principles would be "best guesses" rather than structured infrastructure. The 8:3:8 law provides the methodology for principle creation and refinement.

Finally, principles depend on **Human Commitment (Layer 8)**. Unlike values, which are relatively static, principles must be actively enforced during the build and review process. This requires a human operator who is willing to flag violations and demand rework, even when it is inconvenient. Principles also depend on "Mental Capture"—they must be simple and consistent enough for humans to memorize and apply intuitively during their work.
