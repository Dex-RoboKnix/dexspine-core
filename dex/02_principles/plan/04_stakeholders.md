## Who cares about fundamental principles?

The primary stakeholders for the Principles layer are the developers and LLM agents who are actively building and maintaining the DEX system. For developers, principles provide a clear set of standards that ensure their work is compatible with the rest of the infrastructure. For AI agents, principles serve as the "guardrails" that keep their autonomous actions aligned with the human operator's intent. Both rely on principles to maintain order and prevent "architectural drift."

Architecture reviewers and system auditors are also key stakeholders. They use the principles as a benchmark for evaluating the quality and integrity of new contributions. By checking if a new procedure or process adheres to the core principles (e.g., "Is it composable? Does it use Markdown correctly?"), reviewers can ensure that the system's L0 foundation remains solid and that the "Infrastructure, Not Product" value is being upheld.

Finally, the end-users of the DEX system—humans who interact with the cognitive applications built on top of the spine—are indirect stakeholders. They benefit from the stability and predictability that principles provide. A system built on clear, consistent principles is less likely to produce unexpected or dangerous behaviors. Principles are the silent enablers of a high-quality, trustworthy user experience.
