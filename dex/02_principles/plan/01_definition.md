## What IS fundamental principles of the DEX system?

The Fundamental Principles of the DEX system are the actionable rules and constraints derived directly from the Core Values (Layer 01). While values define the system's "spirit" or identity, principles define its "behavior" and operations. They are the translation of abstract beliefs into enforceable architectural mandates.

Key principles include "Markdown as Wire Protocol," which ensures that all system knowledge is human-readable and machine-parseable. We also adhere to the principle that "Single Agent Beats Multi-Agent"—prioritizing the deep, continuous context of a single spine over the fragmented context of a swarm. Another critical principle is "Human Veto Power Always," ensuring that Layer 8 (the human operator) maintains ultimate authority over all automated actions.

These principles also mandate "L0 Before L1"—the requirement that a solid foundation must be built before tools or applications are developed. By defining these principles in the L0 layer, we provide a consistent set of "Rules of the Road" for all agents and humans who interact with the DEX spine. Principles are the bridges between value and action.
