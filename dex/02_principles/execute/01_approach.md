## How do we implement fundamental principles?

The implementation of Fundamental Principles follows an "Embed, Enforce, Automate" approach. First, we **Embed** the principles directly into the system's architecture and templates. Every new file created within the spine is automatically formatted to follow principles like "Markdown as Wire Protocol" and "1 Task = 1 File." By making the principles the "default state" of the system, we reduce the effort required for humans and agents to stay compliant.

Second, we **Enforce** the principles through a rigorous review process. Every contribution to the spine, whether generated by a human or an agent, must be audited against the L0 principles. If a file violates a naming convention or lacks the necessary cross-references, it is rejected and flagged for rework. This "manual gatekeeping" is essential for maintaining the high-fidelity foundation of the system.

Finally, we **Automate** the checking of principles wherever possible. We use scripts and linters to verify file structures, link integrity, and naming conventions. While the "meaning" of the work still requires human review, the "structure" of the work can be automatically validated. This three-pronged approach ensures that the principles are not just aspirational ideas, but active, enforceable constraints that define the system's daily operation.
