## What gets produced?

The Principles layer produces the **19 Filter Files** and the **DEXFILTER_APPLICATION.md** for this layer. These documents are the "Operational Manual" for the system, defining the rules that all downstream work must follow. Each file is a tangible expression of a principle (e.g., `01_definition.md` defines what principles are, `mve/hu.md` provides the human justification for them). These files are the primary reference for agents during their "Reference Principles" step.

Another key artifact is the **"Principle Reference Card"**—a condensed, one-page version of the core principles that is easily ingestible by LLMs and memorizable by humans. This card acts as the "Checklist of First Resort" for all system operations. By distilling the 19 points into a few punchy mandates, we ensure that the principles are always at the forefront of the system's "working memory."

Finally, this layer produces the **Violation and Exception Logs** (via `CASESTUDY.md` and commit messages). These artifacts track the "Principle Friction" encountered during the build. They are the data source for our improvement process, allowing us to see which principles are working well and which ones are causing unnecessary overhead. The artifacts of the Principles layer are the "Governance Assets" that ensure the system's L0 foundation remains high-fidelity.
